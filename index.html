Meet The Masters

The duo crafting Dholapur's favorite authentic Himalayan street feast

🥟

Anuj

Head Momo Artisan • 18-Pleat Master

Anuj oversees the live bamboo steamers at Dholapur. Utilizing high-hydration artisan dough and the iconic 18-pleat concentric Kathmandu fold, he crafts momos with feather-thin wrappers packed with savory herbs and brothy fillings.

🥟 18-Pleat Fold 🎋 Bamboo Steamed 🥣 Thukpa Clear Broth
🌶️

Shashank

Flavor Alchemist • Dip & Spice Maestro

Shashank crafts the soul of the stall. Slow-roasting red vine tomatoes over coals, charring wild mountain garlic, and stone-crushing white sesame with fierce Himalayan Dalle Khursani peppers, he ensures every bite packs explosive taste.
